Gravity Room Privacy Policy

Developer: TechAppz
Contact: raghunathmamidala.y@gmail.com
Last updated: 11 October 2026

Gameplay progress and settings are saved locally on your device. No player account is required. We do not send your name, email, saved file contents or precise location to analytics services.

Optional usage analytics

If you choose to share usage analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase receives level attempts, successful completions, failures, active play duration, rotations, crystal collection and hint objective/clue-stage events. Firebase also processes installation identifiers, engagement, app/device and operating-system information. Approximate country, region and city may be derived from network information. This is not GPS or live location tracking. We do not request location permissions or enable advertising-ID collection, personalized advertising or Google signals.

Optional crash reporting

If you choose to share crash reports, Firebase Crashlytics receives diagnostic information about crashes and explicitly reported application errors, including stack traces, app/device/operating-system information, installation identifiers and relevant game context such as the current level, graphics setting and last hint. These reports help us fix faults. An ordinary puzzle failure is an analytics event, not an application crash.

Your choices

Both options are initially off. You may accept either, both or neither and still play the complete game. Change choices in Settings → Privacy & data. Disabled usage events are not queued by the game for later reporting. Turning off crash reporting discards pending unsent reports. Turning off collection does not delete reports already uploaded. Contact the developer for privacy inquiries or deletion requests; without a player account we may not be able to identify a particular installation's uploaded data.

Providers and retention

Google processes the selected analytics and diagnostic information under its Firebase/Analytics terms and privacy information. Data may be processed in other countries. Analytics user/event retention is configured to two months; aggregate reports may be retained longer. Crash-report retention follows Firebase Crashlytics' service policy. We do not sell this data. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Audience and updates

Gravity Room is intended for an audience aged 13 and above. These choices do not ask for date of birth. Revisit this policy if the intended audience or collection purposes change. Material changes to optional collection require a new choice before new purposes are enabled.
