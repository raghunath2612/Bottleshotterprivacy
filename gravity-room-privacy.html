Gravity Room Privacy Policy

Developer: TechAppz
Contact: indianapps1999@gmail.com
Last updated: 17 October 2026

Gameplay progress and settings are saved locally on your device. No player account is required. We do not ask for or send your name, email address or mobile number through analytics or crash reporting. We do not upload your saved file contents, contacts or precise GPS location.

Automatic usage analytics

Gravity Room automatically uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and improve its reliability and puzzles. Analytics includes level attempts, successful completions, failures, active play duration, rotations, crystal collection, guide views and hint objective/clue-stage events. Firebase also processes app-instance/installation identifiers, engagement, app/device and operating-system information. Approximate country, region and city may be derived from network information. This is not GPS or live location tracking. We do not request location permissions or enable advertising-ID collection, personalized advertising or Google signals.

Automatic crash reporting

Gravity Room automatically uses Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and explicitly reported application errors. Reports include stack traces, app/device/operating-system information, installation identifiers and relevant game context such as the current level, graphics/FPS setting and last hint. An ordinary puzzle failure is an analytics event, not an application crash.

Collection and offline play

Analytics and crash reporting are enabled automatically, including after updating from an earlier version. There is no in-game consent screen or collection switch. Previous in-game collection preferences no longer control collection in this version. The game remains playable offline; Firebase may store reports locally and transmit them when connectivity returns. Historical gameplay from before collection was enabled is not backfilled.

Identifiers and privacy inquiries

Reports use SDK-generated installation identifiers rather than your name or contact details. These identifiers distinguish installations, so the collected data should not be considered fully anonymous. Contact indianapps1999@gmail.com for privacy inquiries or deletion requests. Without a player account, we may not be able to identify a particular installation's uploaded data. Contact information you voluntarily send in an inquiry is used to respond and is separate from game telemetry.

Providers and retention

Google processes analytics and diagnostic information under its Firebase/Analytics terms and privacy information. Data may be processed in other countries. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Analytics user/event retention is configured to two months; aggregate reports may be retained longer. Crash-report retention follows Firebase Crashlytics' service policy. We do not sell this data. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Audience and updates

Gravity Room is intended for an audience aged 13 and above. The game does not ask for date of birth. This policy describes version 0.2.30 and later unless superseded. Versions through 0.2.29 retain their original optional, initially-off collection controls. We will update it when our data practices change. The privacy policy remains accessible in Settings.
