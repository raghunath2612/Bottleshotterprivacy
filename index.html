Bottle Shooting Game Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Raghunath (indianapps) provides Bottle Shooting Game, also previously called Bottle Shotter Game, as a free game with advertising. This policy explains data handled by the game and its included services.

Game data

The game does not require an account, name, email address, payment details, or a precise location. High scores are saved locally on your device. Clearing the game's app data removes its locally stored settings and scores.

Advertising and analytics

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play services, and the Unity engine. Included services may automatically receive device and app identifiers, IP addresses used to infer approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic information. This can occur during normal use and is not limited to errors.

Google Mobile Ads uses and shares information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention/security. Firebase Analytics provides usage statistics. These services handle information under their own policies and applicable settings:

  • Google and Google Play services
  • AdMob
  • Firebase
  • Unity

Privacy choices

Where required, the game requests advertising privacy choices through Google's User Messaging Platform. When available, the Privacy options button on the main menu lets you review those choices. The Privacy policy button opens this page.

You can also manage advertising identifiers in Android's privacy settings. Resetting or deleting an advertising ID does not necessarily disable every identifier or analytics feature used by included services.

Storage, retention, and security

Google Mobile Ads encrypts the user data described in its SDK disclosure in transit using TLS; Firebase uses HTTPS. Local game scores remain on the device. Included providers may retain service data according to their policies and account settings. We do not promise that SDK data is processed only temporarily or automatically deleted within 90 days.

Children

The game is intended for ages 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe information relating to a child has been provided through the game, contact us so we can review the request with the relevant service provider. Requirements and privacy choices can vary by country.

Requests and external links

For questions or data-related requests, contact indianapps1999@gmail.com. The game does not offer an account-deletion function because it has no user accounts. Information held by third-party services may require a request through those providers. External websites and advertisements have their own privacy practices.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
